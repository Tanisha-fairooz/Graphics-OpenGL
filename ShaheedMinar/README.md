Scene- Shaheed Minar

Objects: 
Shaheed Minar, Buildings, Trees, Clouds, Sun, Car, Ambulance


Animations: 
Trees swaying, Cloud moving, Car moving, Ambulance Moving


Mouse Interaction: 
Left click to decrease and Right click to increase car speed


Keyboard Internation:  
Left key to toggle day/night, T to toggle tree movement, C to toggle cloud, L and R to change cloud direction, A to toggle ambulance and B to toggle car
